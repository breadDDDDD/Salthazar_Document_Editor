# DocStudio

**DocStudio is a free program for reading and editing your documents and PDFs on your own computer.**

Think of it as a simple mix of **Microsoft Word** (for writing letters, reports and notes) and **Adobe Acrobat** (for working with PDF files), all in one window.

- ✅ **Private:** your files never leave your computer. Nothing is uploaded to the internet.
- ✅ **Works offline:** you don't need an internet connection.
- ✅ **Free and nothing to install:** it's a single program you double-click.
- ✅ **Easy on the eyes:** you can make everything bigger, switch to dark mode, or use high contrast.

---

## What can I do with it?

### 📄 Write and edit documents (like Word)

Open, edit and save these kinds of files:

| File type | What it is |
|---|---|
| **.docx** | Microsoft Word documents |
| **.txt** | Plain text files |
| **.md** | Markdown notes |
| **.html** | Web pages |

You can:
- Write and change text, and make it **bold**, *italic*, underlined, coloured or highlighted
- Change the font and text size
- Use headings and titles to organise your writing
- Make bullet lists and numbered lists
- Add tables, pictures, links and page breaks
- Find a word and replace it with another
- Zoom in and out, and see how many words you've written
- Print your document, or save it as a PDF

### 📕 View and edit PDFs (like Acrobat)

You can:
- **Read** PDFs, jump between pages, and search for words
- **Add your own text** anywhere on a page
- **Highlight** important parts
- **Draw** freehand, or add boxes, circles, lines and arrows
- **Cover up** text you want to hide or change ("Whiteout")
- **Add sticky notes**, pictures and stamps (such as "Approved")
- **Sign** a document: draw your signature with the mouse, or type your name
- **Fill in forms**
- **Organise pages:** turn them, delete them, move them around, or take some pages out into a new PDF
- **Combine** several PDFs into one, or split one PDF into separate pages
- **Turn pictures into a PDF** (for example, photos of receipts)
- **Lock a PDF with a password**, or remove a password you know
- Add a **watermark** (such as "DRAFT") and make the file smaller

## Getting started

### 1. Open DocStudio

Double-click **`DocStudio.exe`**.

A window opens with the **Home** screen. (DocStudio uses Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome to show its window. You don't need to set anything up.)

> **Tip:** You can also drag any document onto `DocStudio.exe` to open it straight away.

### 2. Choose what to do

The Home screen has four big buttons:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **New Word document** | Starts a blank page to write on |
| **Open file…** | Opens a document or PDF from your computer |
| **Create PDF from images** | Puts photos or pictures together into one PDF |
| **Merge PDFs** | Joins several PDFs into one |

Your **recent files** are listed underneath, so you can open them again with one click.

You can also **drag a file from your folder and drop it anywhere** in the DocStudio window to open it.

### 3. Find your way around

- Each open file gets its own **tab** at the top, just like in a web browser. Click a tab to switch between files.
- A **dot (•)** on a tab means you have changes that aren't saved yet.
- Under the tabs is the **toolbar** (also called the "ribbon"). It's split into sections like **Home**, **Insert** and **Edit**. Click a section name to see its buttons.
- **Not sure what a button does?** Hold your mouse over it for a moment and a short explanation appears.
- The bar along the bottom gives helpful hints about what to do next.

### 4. Save your work

Press **Ctrl + S**, or click **Save**.

The first time you save a new file, DocStudio asks where to put it and what to call it.

> **Tip:** To save a copy in another format (for example, a Word file as plain text), use **Save as** (**Ctrl + Shift + S**) and pick the type you want.

### 5. Close DocStudio

Just close the window. If you have unsaved changes, DocStudio reminds you first.

## Common tasks, step by step

### ✍️ Write a letter
1. On the Home screen, click **New Word document**.
2. Start typing.
3. To make a title, select the text and click **Title** or **Heading 1** in the toolbar.
4. Press **Ctrl + S** to save it.

### 🔍 Change a word everywhere in a document
1. Open the document.
2. Press **Ctrl + H** (or click **Replace**).
3. Type the word to find, and the word to replace it with.
4. Click **Replace all**.

### 📝 Add text to a PDF
1. Open the PDF.
2. Click the **Edit** section in the toolbar.
3. Click **Add text**, then click on the page where you want the text.
4. Type your text.
5. Press **Esc** when you're done, then **Ctrl + S** to save.

### ✏️ Change text that's already in a PDF
PDFs don't let you edit their original text directly, so DocStudio uses a simple trick:
1. In **Edit**, choose **Whiteout** and drag a box over the old text to cover it.
2. Choose **Add text** and type the new text on top.

### ✒️ Sign a PDF
1. Open the PDF and go to **Edit**.
2. Click **Sign**.
3. Draw your signature with the mouse (or type your name).
4. Place it on the page, and drag the corner to make it bigger or smaller.

DocStudio remembers your signature, so next time you can reuse it.

### 🗂️ Remove or rearrange pages in a PDF
1. Open the PDF. Small pictures of every page appear on the left.
2. Click a page (hold **Ctrl** to pick several).
3. Go to the **Organize** section and choose **Rotate**, **Delete**, **Extract**, or move pages up and down.

You can also drag the small page pictures to put them in a new order.

### 📸 Turn photos into a PDF
1. On the Home screen, click **Create PDF from images**.
2. Pick your pictures (PNG or JPG).
3. Save the new PDF.

### 🔒 Put a password on a PDF
1. Open the PDF and go to **Protect**.
2. Click the password option and type a password.
3. Save the file.

> ⚠️ **Write the password down somewhere safe.** If you forget it, there's no way to open the file again.

## Making it easier to see and use

Click the **⚙ (Settings)** button in the top-right corner. You can:

- **Make everything bigger:** use the size slider (up to double size).
- **Change the colours:** Light, Dark, or **High contrast** (bold colours that are easier to tell apart).
- **Use an easier-to-read font:** helpful for people with dyslexia.
- **Turn off animations:** if moving things on screen bother you.

DocStudio also works **fully without a mouse**: press **Tab** to move between buttons and **Enter** to press one. It also works with screen readers.

## Handy keyboard shortcuts

Press **F1** at any time (or click the **?** button) to see the full list.

| Press | To |
|---|---|
| **Ctrl + N** | Start a new document |
| **Ctrl + O** | Open a file |
| **Ctrl + S** | Save |
| **Ctrl + Shift + S** | Save as (choose a new name or format) |
| **Ctrl + W** | Close the current file |
| **Ctrl + Z** / **Ctrl + Y** | Undo / redo |
| **Ctrl + F** | Find a word |
| **Ctrl + Tab** | Switch to the next open file |
| **Alt + Home** | Go back to the Home screen |
| **Esc** | Cancel, or close a pop-up window |

**In documents:** Ctrl + B (bold), Ctrl + I (italic), Ctrl + U (underline), Ctrl + K (add a link), Ctrl + H (find and replace).

**In PDFs, choose a tool with one key:** **V** select, **T** add text, **H** highlight, **D** draw, **W** whiteout, **N** sticky note, **R** rectangle, **E** ellipse, **L** line, **A** arrow, **S** sign.

## Questions and problems

**Is my information sent anywhere?**
No. Everything happens on your own computer, and DocStudio works without the internet.

**The window didn't open.**
Make sure Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome is installed (Edge comes with Windows). Wait a few seconds and double-click `DocStudio.exe` again.

**My Word document looks a bit different in DocStudio.**
DocStudio handles everyday documents well: text, headings, lists, tables and pictures. Very fancy layouts (columns, text boxes, headers and footers) may not appear exactly as they do in Word. Keep a copy of important originals before editing.

**I can't select or change the original words in a PDF.**
That's normal for PDFs. See *"Change text that's already in a PDF"* above.

**How do I close DocStudio completely?**
Just close the window. DocStudio switches itself off within a few seconds — nothing keeps running in the background.

<details>
<summary><b>For developers (technical details)</b></summary>

DocStudio is written in Go. It runs a small local web server on `127.0.0.1`, which only accepts requests from its own window, and shows its interface in an Edge or Chrome app window.

**Build:** `go build -ldflags "-H=windowsgui" -o DocStudio.exe .`
**Run with a console for debugging:** `go run . -no-browser -port 8080`, then open http://127.0.0.1:8080
**Test:** `go test ./...`

| Folder / file | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `main.go` | Local server; bundles the `web/` folder into the program |
| `internal/fsapi` | Browse, read and save files |
| `internal/docx` | Converts Word files to and from HTML (standard library only) |
| `internal/pdfops` | PDF operations, using [pdfcpu](https://github.com/pdfcpu/pdfcpu) |
| `web/` | Interface: `app.js` (main window), `word.js` (document editor), `pdfeditor.js` (PDF editor), `vendor/pdfjs` (PDF display, pdf.js 3.11.174) |

</details>
